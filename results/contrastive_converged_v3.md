## Design choices

1. Stopping rule: the PPO warm-start rule (daniel/convergence.py), checked at every validation (every 50 updates): stop once the best validation HV has improved by less than 0.5% (relative) over the last 1,000 updates, after at least 1,000 updates. Hard cap 8,000 updates; a run that reaches it is reported as capped, not converged.
2. Step 1: the rule was replayed on each fixed-budget run's logged validation history before any run (frozen in configs/contrastive_converged_v3.json). If it never stopped the history, the arm continues from its saved final state; if it would have stopped it earlier, the arm reruns from its saved starting network.
3. A continued run loads the saved weights, optimiser, RNG states, logs, best checkpoint and generator counters, and may differ from its source only in the model name and the stopping rule (otherwise train.py refuses). Before continuing, the resumed policy must reproduce the last logged validation HV exactly; otherwise the arm reruns from its starting network (exit code 3, recorded).
4. Two fixed-budget runs ended between two instance resamplings (update 3,350; resampling every 20 updates), and their resume states predate the saved instance batch: the batch is rebuilt by replaying the instance generator from the seed, accepted only if the replay ends in exactly the saved numpy state and generator counters. Resume states now also store the batch.
5. Wall-clock and environment steps of a continued run include its fixed-budget part (same machine, --jobs 3); a rerun counts from zero.
6. Contrastive settings unchanged (configs/contrastive_v3.json); validation, checkpoint selection and evaluation as before.
7. Regret is computed over the converged and the fixed-budget models together (plus the specialists, the rules and CP-SAT B1/B2), so its values differ from the earlier reports; HV, IGD+ and unique non-dominated counts of the fixed-budget models are the earlier values.
