# World-model trunk, Milestone 1 (10x5): does predictive pretraining teach the trunk transferable factory knowledge?

Protocol: `docs/world_model_m1_protocol_v3.md`. Generated by `tools/analyze_world_model_m1_v3.py`. **Single pretraining seed: exploratory evidence only.** No specialists, merging or RL. The evaluation split and the reserved final-test sets were never used.

## Outcome: **INCONCLUSIVE**

Rule that decided it: neither FAIL nor PASS: more than 5% worse than the shared base on tardiness.

The rule was fixed before the run (`configs/world_model_m1_v3.json`; MLP probe, mean over probe seeds):
- **FAIL:** the world model loses to the mean or shortcut predictor on 2 or more of the 3 outcome groups, OR its probe is not better than the random trunk's on any objective.
- **PASS:** sanity passes AND its probe error is at least 5% lower than the equal-weight shared base's on at least 2 of 3 objectives, with no objective more than 5% worse.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** anything else.
- The makespan-specialist trunk is context only and does not enter the rule.

**Against the shared base** (MLP probe MAE, (world model − base) / base): final makespan -45.1%, carbon -13.4%, tardiness +21.6%.

## Question A: does the world model predict outcomes? (v3 validation instances, the 3 pretraining continuations)

Mean absolute error in raw units, lower is better. **Mean:** the training-data mean of each output, per continuation rule. **Shortcut:**
- *Job completion:* DANIEL's earliest-possible-completion estimate after the action (the environment's lower bound).
- *Machines:* the committed busy time or energy, plus every unscheduled operation on its fastest or lowest-energy machine. DANIEL has no machine estimate, so this analogue is used.

## Question B: frozen-trunk transfer to an unseen continuation (least slack + ECT)

Each trunk is frozen. A small probe is trained on the 32-value state-action embedding (the candidate operation's and machine's embeddings plus the two global means; no raw features) to predict the outcome when the schedule is finished by least-slack + ECT, a rule never seen in pretraining.
- **Instances:** probe-train [0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16] (of which [16] are used for early stopping) and probe-test [1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17], by validation-instance index.
- **Samples:** 5,584 fitting, 6,219 test.
- **Reported:** test MAE in normalised units (makespan / 990, carbon / 668.2 kg, tardiness / Tref 557.4), mean ± std over 5 probe seeds. Pairwise accuracy is the share of same-state pairs of candidate actions that the probe orders correctly.

## Design choices

- **Trunk:** DANIEL's dual-attention encoder (`feature_exact`), the same architecture and size as the specialists. The pretrained trunk is saved as a full DANIEL bundle (fresh seed-111 actor and critic, schema `benchmark_v3_f12_m9_p10`), so it loads into a specialist (`--init_mode exact`) or a preference-conditioned DANIEL (`--init_mode backbone`).
- **Readout:** the action is read out of the trunk's embeddings exactly as DANIEL's actor reads it: [chosen job's candidate operation, chosen machine, mean operation, mean machine] embedding.
  - *Job head:* [that job's candidate-operation embedding, action embedding, continuation one-hot] → the job's completion time; applied to every job.
  - *Machine head:* [that machine's embedding, action embedding, continuation one-hot] → busy time and energy; applied to every machine.
  - The continuation rule enters only the heads, never the trunk.
- **Training:** Huber loss on targets normalised by fixed constants (times / 990 = 99 × N/M; energy / 133.65 = 13.365 kg × N/M). Adam 1e-3, 256 states per batch with all their candidate actions and continuations. 10% of the generated instances are held out by instance; early stop after 5 epochs without improvement on that hold-out, cap 100.
- **Data:**
  - *Behaviour policies* (one per episode, drawn at random): MWKR+ECT, MWKR+MINE, EDD+ECT, uniform random, and random 30% / MWKR+ECT 70%.
  - *States:* 6 random decision steps per episode.
  - *Actions:* the behaviour's choice plus up to 3 other allowed actions.
  - *Continuations:* each (state, action) is finished by MWKR+ECT, MWKR+MINE and EDD+ECT under DANIEL's own no-waiting rule, using the tested copy of the environment's transition.
  - *Targets:* exact.
- **Validation data:** every validation instance is run under all five behaviours, every decision step is used, and every continuation includes least slack + ECT.
- **The random-initialisation trunk** equals the world model's own starting trunk (same seed, same construction).
- **Single pretraining seed: exploratory evidence only.**
